Death is only the beginning.

HahUeuh brings Re:Zero into Minecraft. When you die, the world turns back to how it was and nobody but you remembers. Your character is born with a Gate, a set of stats, and whatever blessings fate happened to hand out.

What the mod does

HahUeuh doesn't add a questline to follow. It changes how dying, fighting and casting work, then leaves you to find your own story inside those rules.

Two things surprise most new players. The first is that you are probably not a mage: three in four characters are born with no magic at all. The second is that dying doesn't just cost you your stuff, it costs everyone on the server the last few minutes of their lives.

Return by Death

The world quietly saves itself while you play. Die, and it all snaps back. The blocks you broke, the mobs you killed, the time of day, everyone else. Only you remember.

Authorities

The powers of the Witch Cult. Greed and Sloth each come in three completely different flavours, and which one you get is down to luck.

Divine Protections

Thirty-nine blessings handed out at birth. Most players get none. A lucky few get one that changes how they play entirely.

Magic

Sixteen spells, all of them Yin. Push more than your Gate can handle and the spell fizzles. Push too hard for too long and your Gate breaks for good.

Spirits

Make a contract with an elemental spirit and it lends you its magic. For most characters this is the only way into casting at all.

Stats

Six stats that grow by being used. Each one has a hidden ceiling you were born with, and no amount of training gets you past it.

Witch Factors

The opposite of a blessing. Pick one up and every Divine Protection you own burns away. You can never hold both.

Where to start

Read Getting started first. It covers installing the mod and what happens the moment you join a world, which is when everything about your character gets decided. After that, Book of Life shows you how to read your own character sheet.

Numbers on this wiki are the defaults. Nearly everything can be changed by whoever runs your server, so what you see in game might not match. If something seems off, ask them, or check Configuration.
