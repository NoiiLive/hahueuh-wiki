Death is only the beginning.

HahUeuh brings the world of Re:Zero into Minecraft — the world rewinds when you die, the Witch Cult's Authorities are yours to wield, and every character is born with a Gate, a set of stats and whatever Divine Protections fate happened to grant them.

What the mod does

HahUeuh is a systems mod rather than a content pack. It does not add a questline to follow — it adds interlocking mechanics that change how dying, fighting and casting work, and then lets players find their own stories inside them.

Return by Death

The world silently checkpoints as you play. Die, and everything rewinds — terrain, mobs, other players, the time of day. Only you remember.

Authorities

Greed's Domain, Sloth's Unseen Hands, and more. Each Authority is a family of active abilities with its own costs and cooldowns.

Divine Protections

Thirty-nine blessings across four rarity tiers, rolled at birth. Three in four players get none at all.

Magic

Sixteen Yin spells gated behind your Gate's Output and Efficiency. Overreach and you strain it; strain it enough and it breaks for good.

Spirit Arts

Commune with elemental spirits, bind them to anchors, and let them fund spells from schools you were never born with.

Stats & growth

Six stats, each with a rolled proficiency and capacity that decide how fast you learn and how far you can ever get.

Witch Factors

The dark mirror of a Divine Protection. Gaining one burns away every blessing you hold — the two can never coexist.
