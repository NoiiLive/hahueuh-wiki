Book of Life

Your character sheet. Most of what the world decided about you when you first joined is written here, though not all of it.

The five lines at the top

LineWhat it tells you
RaceHuman, Elf or Half Elf. Everyone starts Human. See Races.
AgeStarts at 16, goes up every thirty in game days.
GateOpen, Partly Open, Damaged, Destroyed or Defective.
ManaWhat you have and what you can hold. Refills by gathering.
OdWhat you have and what you can hold. Only refills when you die.
A broken Gate doesn't say how it's broken. The book just says Defective. Whether you can't absorb mana or can't release it is something you work out by trying, or by asking an admin to look it up.

The stat bars

Six bars, one per stat. The number above each is your current level and the bar shows how close you are to the next one.

Hover a bar and you'll see the two hidden numbers behind it: how fast that stat grows for you, and how high it can ever go. Both were set when your character was born and neither will change.

Green and red numbers

When something is temporarily changing a stat, the big number shows the boosted total rather than your real level. Green means something is pushing it up. Red means something is dragging it down. Hover to see the split.

Blessings like Shedding Blood, Fireplay, Sun, Moon, Unarmed Combat and Weapon God all do this, as does being tutored by another player.

What it deliberately hides

  • Spirit Affinity. Rolled 1 to 10 and never shown anywhere. It decides whether spirits will deal with you at all.
  • Which kind of broken Gate you have, if yours is Defective.
  • How close you are to an Authority. Greed and Sloth both track a hidden score.

Your blessings aren't in the book either. They live in the ability menu, and anyone with the Judgement blessing can read yours straight off you.
