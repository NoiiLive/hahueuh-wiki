Book of Life

Your character sheet. Everything the world decided about you when you first joined is written here.

The five fields

FieldShows
RaceHuman, Elf or Half Elf. See Races.
AgeStarts at 16, rises every 30 in-game days.
GateOpen, Partly Open, Damaged, Destroyed or Defective.
ManaCurrent and maximum. Refills by charging.
OdCurrent and maximum. Only refills on death.
A Defective Gate does not say which kind it is. The Book reports it simply as Defective. Whether you have No Absorption or No Release is something you find out by trying — or by asking an admin to check.

The stat bars

Six bars, one per stat. The number above each bar is your current effective level; the bar shows progress toward the next one.

Hovering a bar breaks it down into proficiency (how fast it grows) and capacity (how high it can ever go), both rolled 1–10 at birth.

Temporary boosts

When something is temporarily raising or lowering a stat — Shedding Blood, Fireplay, Sun, Moon, Unarmed Combat, Weapon God, or a Tutoring multiplier — the main number shows the boosted total, coloured to show the direction:

ColourMeaning
GreenCurrently boosted above your trained level.
RedCurrently reduced below it.

The tooltip separates the two, so you can always see what you have actually trained underneath the bonus.

What it does not show

Deliberately hidden from your own sheet:

  • Spirit Affinity — rolled 1–10 and never displayed.
  • Which Defective variant you have.
  • Authority compatibility — how close you are to Greed or Sloth.

Your Divine Protections are not in the Book either; they appear in the ability menu, and someone with the Divine Protection of Judgement can read yours off you directly.
