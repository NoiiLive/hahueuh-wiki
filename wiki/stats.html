Stats

Six stats, each rolled at birth with a ceiling you can never train past. They level by being used — you cannot spend points.

Proficiency, capacity and level

Every stat carries three numbers. Confusing them is the most common mistake players make.

NumberRangeWhat it does
Proficiency1–10How fast the stat gains experience. Rolled at birth.
Capacity1–10How high the level may ever go. Rolled at birth.
Level0–100What you have actually trained.

The level ceiling is capacity × 10. A stat rolled at capacity 3 stops at level 30 forever; capacity 10 allows level 100. Each level takes 100 progress, and proficiency multiplies how much progress each action grants.

Your starting level is not zero. Stats begin at a multiple of their own roll, between 1× and 2×, so a strong roll starts ahead as well as ending higher.

What each stat changes

Every bonus below is the value at maximum for that stat — you receive a fraction of it proportional to how far along you are. All are configurable defaults.

StatAt maximum
Tenacity +112.5% max health, and +150% healing received.
Fortitude 52.5% incoming damage reduction.
Strength +3 fist damage, +60% jump height, 75% less fall damage.
Reflexes +37.5% movement speed, and +75% attack speed while unarmed.
Magic +150% mana capacity, +150% charge rate, and +75% to Gate Output and Efficiency.
Combat +90% damage and +52.5% attack speed — but only with a weapon in hand.
Armed and unarmed use different stats. With a weapon, your damage and attack speed come from Combat. Bare-handed, they come from Strength and Reflexes instead. This is also how the Unarmed Combat and Weapon God protections split.

How stats level

Experience is awarded for doing the thing the stat governs. There is no other way to raise a stat.

The Divine Protection of Training multiplies all of it. The Divine Protection of Instruction lets another player grant you a multiplier for one specific stat.

Temporary bonuses

Several protections push a stat above its normal value for a while — Shedding Blood as you lose health, Fireplay while you burn, Sun and Moon by time of day, Unarmed Combat and Weapon God by what you are holding. In the Book of Life the boosted total is the number shown, and the tooltip breaks out where the extra came from.

Bonuses may exceed the cap. Temporary boosts are deliberately allowed past your capacity ceiling. The trained level underneath is untouched.
