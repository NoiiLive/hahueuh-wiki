Stats

Six stats that grow by being used. You can't spend points on them, and each one has a ceiling you were born with.

The three numbers on every stat

This trips up nearly everyone. Each stat has a level, which is what you've trained, and two hidden numbers that were set when your character was born.

  • Your level is what you've actually earned. It's the big number on the bar, and it can reach 100 at most.
  • Proficiency decides how fast that level goes up. Rolled from 1 to 10.
  • Capacity decides how high it can ever go. Also rolled from 1 to 10.

Your ceiling is your capacity times ten. Roll a 3 in Magic and you stop at level 30 for the rest of that character's life, no matter how long you train. Roll a 10 and you can reach 100.

You don't start at zero. Every stat begins somewhere between one and two times its own roll, so a good roll starts you ahead as well as letting you finish higher.

What each stat gives you

The numbers below are what you get at maximum. Along the way you receive a share of it proportional to how far you've come.

StatAt maximum
TenacityMore than double your health, and healing works half again as well on you.
FortitudeEverything that hits you does about half damage.
StrengthPunch for 3 more, jump 60% higher, take a quarter of the fall damage.
ReflexesMove much faster, and swing much faster when your hands are empty.
MagicTwo and a half times the mana, gather it far quicker, and a stronger Gate.
CombatAlmost double damage and much faster swings, but only with a weapon.
Armed and unarmed use different stats. Holding a weapon, your damage and speed come from Combat. Bare handed they come from Strength and Reflexes. This is also why the Unarmed Combat and Weapon God blessings are two separate things.

How to train them

You train a stat by doing the thing it covers. There's no other way.

Do thisTrains
Hit things with a weaponCombat
Hit things with your fistsStrength
Break blocks by handStrength
Take hits while wearing armourFortitude
Block with a shieldFortitude
Take hits with no armour onTenacity
Get healedTenacity
SprintReflexes
Spend mana, or gather itMagic

Two blessings speed this up. Training makes everything you do count for more. Instruction lets another player boost one particular stat of yours.

Temporary boosts

Several blessings push a stat above its normal value for a while. Shedding Blood does it as you lose health, Fireplay while you're burning, Sun and Moon depending on the time of day, and Unarmed Combat and Weapon God depending on what you're holding.

When that's happening, the Book of Life shows the boosted number in green, and the tooltip splits out how much of it is real training and how much is temporary.

Boosts can go over your ceiling. That's deliberate. Your trained level underneath is untouched, so nothing is wasted.
