Stats

Six stats, each with a rolled ceiling you can never train past.

Proficiency, capacity and level

Each stat carries three numbers, and confusing them is the most common mistake players make.

  • Proficiency \x{2014} how quickly the stat gains experience. Rolled at birth.
  • Capacity \x{2014} how high the stat's level may ever go. Rolled at birth.
  • Level \x{2014} what you have actually trained, capped by capacity.

Proficiency and capacity are fixed for life under normal play. A character born with low capacity in Magic will never be a great caster no matter how long they train \x{2014} that is the point.

Training

Stats level by being used. The Divine Protection of Training multiplies all stat experience you earn; the Divine Protection of Instruction lets someone else grant you a multiplier for a specific stat.

Temporary bonuses

Several protections push a stat above its normal value for a while \x{2014} Shedding Blood as you lose health, Fireplay while you burn, Sun and Moon by time of day. In the Book of Life the boosted total is what you see; the tooltip breaks out where the extra came from.

Bonuses can exceed the cap. Temporary boosts are deliberately allowed past your capacity ceiling. Your trained level is untouched underneath.
