Sloth

Invisible hands that reach out to hit things, grab things and carry you around. All three variants share that core. What separates them is reach, appearance, and one extra trick each.

The three variants

VariantChanceReachExtra ability
Invisible Providence55%16 blocksHidden Interaction
Unseen Hands40%16 blocksSelf Propel, and Finger Grant
Sekhmet5%8 blocksNone

A mob with Sloth gets an even chance of any of the three rather than following these odds.

What all three can do

Every variant gets Summon Hand, with a ten second cooldown, plus Quick Strike and Quick Grasp at five seconds each.

The hands hit as hard as you do. Your weapon, your Strength effects and any Weakness on you all carry through to what they deal, so buffing yourself buffs your hands.

Invisible Providence

The most common variant, and the hardest to see coming. It gains Hidden Interaction, and its hands are the least visible of the three.

Unseen Hands

Gains Self Propel, which lets you throw yourself around using the hands, and Finger Grant for sharing the power.

How many hands you get is fixed for your character, somewhere between five and fifteen. It's worked out from your player ID, so it never changes and rerolling isn't possible.

Sekhmet

The rarest at one in twenty. Its hands are enormous and mounted at the shoulders, with the exact size again fixed to your character.

The trade is real: Sekhmet's reach is half everyone else's, eight blocks instead of sixteen, and it has no extra ability to make up for it. It's the flashiest variant and the weakest.

Fingers

Finger Grant belongs to Unseen Hands alone, with a 30 second cooldown. It gives part of the Authority to somebody else. That person becomes a Finger and gets their own Summon Hand, Quick Strike, Quick Grasp and Self Propel.

A Finger's abilities don't appear until the hands do. They show up based on actually having hands rather than on the Authority, so a new Finger's menu stays empty until the grant lands.

Earning Sloth

Sloth rewards letting other things do the work for you. A tamed pet getting a kill is worth the most, sleeping through the night is worth less, and travelling by boat or minecart is worth least. See Authorities.

Mobs with Sloth use their quick abilities roughly one time in seven.
