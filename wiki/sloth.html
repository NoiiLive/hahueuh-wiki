Sloth

Invisible limbs that strike, grasp and carry you. Three variants share a core set of abilities but differ in reach, appearance, and one exclusive power each.

A mob given Sloth rolls all three variants with equal probability rather than by these weights.

Shared abilities

All three variants get these. Hands reach 16 blocks by default — 8 for Sekhmet, which is halved.

AbilityCooldown
Summon Hand10s

Hand damage scales with your own attack power — weapon attributes, Strength effects and Weakness all carry through to what the hands hit for.

Variant-exclusive abilities

Invisible Providence

Gains Hidden Interaction. Its hands are the least visible of the three.

Unseen Hands

Gains Self Propel — using the hands to move yourself, at a default mobility speed of 8 — and Finger Grant.

An Unseen Hands holder has a randomised number of hands, between 5 and 15, derived from their UUID so it is stable for that character.

Sekhmet

The rarest variant at 5%. Its hands are large — a size between 3 and 6, again derived from UUID — mounted at the shoulders, but their reach is halved. It has no exclusive ability of its own.

Fingers

Finger Grant (30 second cooldown) is exclusive to Unseen Hands. It hands a portion of the Authority to someone else — the recipient becomes a Finger and receives their own Summon Hand, Quick Strike, Quick Grasp and Self Propel.

Finger abilities appear only once you actually have hands. They are gated on holding hands rather than on the Sloth variant, so a Finger's menu stays empty until the grant lands. The hand count is server-authoritative.

Earning Sloth

Sloth compatibility rewards letting something else do the work: 5 points when a tamed pet gets a kill, 2 for sleeping through the night, 1 for travelling by vehicle. At 100 the Authority becomes available.

Sloth-holding mobs use their quick actions with a 15% chance.
