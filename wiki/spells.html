Spell list

Sixteen spells, plus two General skills. Every one of them is Yin.

Only Yin has spells right now. Fire, Water, Earth, Wind and Yang exist as schools — they can be rolled at birth, granted by an affinity protection, and lent by a spirit — but no spell has been written for them yet. Being born with Fire currently gives you nothing to cast.

All numbers below are the shipped defaults from the Yin magic config. Every one is adjustable, so a server may not match. Total is the whole mana cost of the cast; per tick is the rate it is drawn at, which is what your Gate's Output is checked against to decide whether the cast succeeds or fizzles.

Al Shamak10009120s Banishes a target into a pocket dimension for 15s, taking everything within 10 blocks of them along. Non-players can be held indefinitely instead, and it doubles as spell storage.

A sealed target cannot be banished. Al Shamak refuses anyone already held by Ol Shamak, and skips sealed bystanders when it sweeps up the group around its target.

Gravity — Vita and Murak

These three are opposites and share a pattern: shift while casting to apply it to yourself indefinitely, or cast it normally at a target you are looking at. Casting it on yourself again turns it off.

SpellTotalPer tickCooldownEffect
Vita200310s Increases gravity on a target within 24 blocks — 1.5× gravity and 1.5× fall damage.
El Vita500520s 2× gravity and fall damage. A fall of 5 blocks or more leaves a crater.
Murak200310s The reverse — reduces gravity to near nothing and grants drifting flight, with a gust every 2 seconds.
Vita is not a healing spell. Despite the name, nothing in this mod's Vita line restores health.

Sustained — E.M.M. and E.M.T.

Both are toggles rather than one-shot casts. You pay the cast cost to raise them, then an upkeep every second until you drop them or run dry. Casting again ends them early, and re-casting has no cooldown while active.

SpellTotalPer tickUpkeepCooldownEffect
E.M.M.600750/s60s Stillness on yourself. While it is up, no other spell may be cast.
E.M.T.700850/s90s Anchors a silence field of 25 block radius where you cast it. Anyone inside is cut off from casting and from charging mana.
Spirits pay the upkeep too. The per-second drain is taken from your spirit pools first and only then from your own mana, exactly like the cast itself.
