Spell list

Sixteen spells, plus two skills that aren't really spells. All sixteen belong to Yin.

Only Yin has spells so far. Fire, Water, Earth, Wind and Yang all exist as schools. You can be born with them, be granted them by a blessing, or borrow them from a spirit. There just isn't anything written for them to cast yet. If you rolled Fire, you currently have nothing to show for it.

Two numbers matter for each spell. Cost is the total mana it takes. Speed is how fast that mana gets pulled out of you, and it's the number your Gate's Output is measured against to decide whether the spell works or fizzles. If a spell keeps failing, that's why. See The Gate.

Shamak: darkness and shutting people down

Shamak

An ink cloud spreads 16 blocks around you and blinds everything caught in it for 10 seconds. Mobs inside forget what they were attacking. Costs 100 mana at a speed of 3, with a 15 second cooldown.

El Shamak

A tighter cloud, 10 blocks, but instead of blinding people it disconnects them from their own bodies for 8 seconds. Also wipes mob targeting. Costs 300 at speed 5, 20 second cooldown.

Ul Shamak

Drops a black hole wherever you're looking, up to 20 blocks away. It drags things in from 12 blocks, deals 3 damage at its centre, eats the terrain, and lasts 6 seconds. Costs 500 at speed 7, one minute cooldown.

Al Karum

The same black hole, much bigger. Pulls from 24 blocks, hits for 6, and chews through terrain twice as fast. Costs 1000 at speed 8, 90 second cooldown.

Al Shamak

Banishes whoever you target into a pocket dimension for 15 seconds, and takes everything within 10 blocks of them along for the ride. Mobs can be held there indefinitely rather than released, and the same spell doubles as a way to store another spell for later. Costs 1000 at speed 9, two minute cooldown.

Ol Shamak

Seals someone up to 24 blocks away for a full minute. They can't move, act, or be moved by anyone else. The seal has 40 health, so allies can break it early by attacking it. Costs 1250 at speed 10, 90 second cooldown.

You can't banish someone who's already sealed. Al Shamak refuses anyone held by Ol Shamak, and skips sealed bystanders when it sweeps up everyone around its target.

Minya: ice stakes

Minya

Three stakes form around you and fly at whatever you're looking at, 6 damage each. They leave the target crystallised for a couple of seconds, and if that crystal shatters it deals another 20% and throws homing shards. Costs 150 at speed 3, 8 second cooldown.

El Minya

Fifteen stakes instead of three, at 4 damage each. Land three of them within a second and they detonate for 8 damage across 4 blocks. Costs 300 at speed 5, 15 second cooldown.

Ul Minya

Locks a single target in a ring for 3 seconds and then hits them for 40. You need a target within 24 blocks to cast it. Costs 1000 at speed 8, one minute cooldown.

Vita and Murak: messing with gravity

These three work the same way. Cast normally to affect whoever you're looking at, or hold shift while casting to affect yourself for as long as you like. Casting it on yourself again switches it off.

Vita

Makes a target heavier. Gravity and fall damage both go up by half. Costs 200 at speed 3, 10 second cooldown.

El Vita

The stronger version. Gravity and fall damage both double, and anyone who falls more than five blocks leaves a crater where they land. Costs 500 at speed 5, 20 second cooldown.

Murak

The opposite. Gravity almost disappears and the target drifts, getting a small push every couple of seconds. Costs 200 at speed 3, 10 second cooldown.

Vita isn't a healing spell. The name suggests it, but nothing in the Vita line restores health.

Getting around

Teleportation

Costs 200 to start, plus 5 more for every block you travel. Moving yourself adds another 100. Instead of travelling you can open a gateway that stays put for 15 seconds, which costs 200 extra and lets other people walk through. One minute cooldown.

Door Crossing

Look at a door within 6 blocks and it becomes an entrance to a private room, 75 blocks across and 32 high, for the next 15 seconds. Other doors within 100 blocks have a 1 in 10 chance of quietly leading there too. Costs 700 at speed 5, 30 second cooldown.

E.M.M. and E.M.T.: spells you hold up

These two aren't cast and forgotten. You pay to raise them, then keep paying 50 mana every second until you drop them or run out. Casting again ends it early, and there's no cooldown while it's still up.

E.M.M.

Locks you in stillness. While it's up you can't cast anything else. Costs 600 to raise at speed 7, one minute cooldown once it ends.

E.M.T.

Plants a silence field 25 blocks across where you cast it. Anyone standing in it can't cast and can't gather mana. Costs 700 at speed 8, 90 second cooldown.

Your spirits help pay the upkeep. That 50 per second comes out of your spirits first and only then out of your own mana, the same as a normal cast.

The two skills that aren't spells

Spirit Commune and Temporary Contract cost mana but don't belong to any school, so even a No Release Gate can use them. Neither one can fizzle, no matter how low your Output is. See Spirits.

What mobs can cast

Magic-using mobs each roll for which spells they know. A mob is most likely to have Minya and least likely to have Al Karum.
