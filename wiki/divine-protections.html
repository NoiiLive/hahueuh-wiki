Divine Protections

Thirty-nine blessings, handed out by the world at birth. Most are quiet passives you may never notice; a handful rewrite what your character is capable of, and five of those can only ever belong to one person at a time.

How you get them

Protections are rolled once, the first time you join a world, alongside your race, stats and Gate. You do not choose them and you cannot train into them. Each rarity tier is rolled separately with its own chance, so it is perfectly possible to be born with three Rare protections and no Common one.

Rolling only ever draws from the four tier pools below. A protection that is not listed in any pool cannot be rolled at all — which is how od_lagna is normally kept out of circulation on a fresh server.

Uniques are never stolen by a roll. Once someone holds a unique protection it is removed from the rolling pool entirely, so nobody else can roll into it. It only changes hands through commands, the Usurpation active, or the original holder's death on a hardcore world.

Losing a protection

Protections are permanent under normal play. They can leave you in four ways:

  • An admin removes it with a command.
  • Someone uses Usurpation on you.
  • You gain a Witch Factor — which burns every Divine Protection you hold away. The two can never coexist.
  • You die on a hardcore world, which releases any unique you were holding back into the world.

Phoenix is the exception: it is spent on use, and only stays if its roll succeeds.
