The Gate

Everyone has a Gate. It's the thing magic passes through on its way out of you, and its condition decides whether you can cast at all.

The five states

Your Gate is in one of these, and you can see which in the Book of Life.

StateWhat it means
OpenHealthy. This is what you start with if you were born knowing magic.
Partly OpenWhat you start with if you weren't. It still works.
DamagedYou've overused it. Still works, but you're one step from ruining it.
DestroyedYou can't cast and you can't gather mana. It never heals.
DefectiveBorn broken. Roughly 1 character in 20 gets this no matter what else they rolled.

Being born with a broken Gate

A Defective Gate isn't just a weak one. It's broken in a particular direction, and there are two, each about as likely as the other. The Book of Life won't tell you which one you have, so you find out by trying.

No Absorption

You can't pull mana in from the world around you. Whatever you're carrying is all you're ever going to have, so every point of it counts.

No Release

You can't push magic out. Every spell in the six schools is refused, including ones a spirit was going to pay for, because the magic still has to leave through your Gate.

No Release isn't the same as having no magic. Anything that just costs mana still works. You can still commune with spirits and make contracts, both the permanent kind and the temporary kind. It's only actual spellcasting that's blocked.

Output and Efficiency

Your Gate has two ratings. Both are set when your character is born, somewhere from 1 to 10, and both improve as you train the Magic stat.

Output is how hard you can push. It sets how fast you cast, and it decides whether a spell works at all. Efficiency is how little you waste. Poor Efficiency never stops you casting, it just makes everything cost more mana than it should.

Why spells fizzle

Every spell has a speed it wants to be cast at. If that's more than three times what your Output can manage, the spell fails and the mana is gone.

In practice, with an Output of 3 you can handle spells that draw up to 8 per tick. Al Shamak draws 9, so it will fail every single time until you either train Magic or get a spirit to help.

The size limit on spells

Separately from all that, your Magic level puts a hard cap on how big a spell you're allowed to attempt on your own. Go over it and the spell is refused before it starts.

Magic levelBiggest spellWhat that gets you
Under 15250Shamak, Minya, Vita, Murak, Teleportation
15 to 39500El Shamak, El Minya, Ul Shamak, El Vita
40 to 79750E.M.M., E.M.T., Door Crossing
80 and up1500Al Shamak, Al Karum, Ul Minya, Ol Shamak
A spirit can lift you past your own limit. Spirits carry a size limit of their own, and the game uses whichever is higher. A Great spirit is worth as much as being level 80, which is how a new character ever gets to cast the big spells.

Strain, and how Gates break

Strain is a hidden counter from 0 to 100. You build it by holding more mana than you're supposed to. You get 50% over your limit for free, and past that you gain strain steadily for as long as you stay overloaded.

It fades on its own over about twenty minutes. The danger is what happens while it's high:

StrainWhat happens
75Your Gate becomes Damaged. You'll hear glass break.
100Your Gate is Destroyed. You'll hear a heartbeat.
Destroyed is forever. Letting the strain fade back to zero doesn't bring the Gate back. Nothing in normal play repairs it. Only an admin command can undo this.
Not every Gate can break this way. Only Open and Damaged Gates degrade. If yours is Partly Open or Defective, strain can't destroy it. You're also safe while in creative mode, or while frozen by Lion's Heart.

Blessings that change your Gate

A few Divine Protections set your Gate the moment you receive them, then leave it alone. After that it behaves like anyone else's, so it can still be strained, damaged and destroyed.

BlessingSets your Gate to
SorceryOpen, no strain, Output and Efficiency both maxed at 10
Any elemental affinityOpen
