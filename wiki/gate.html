The Gate

The organ every caster channels through \x{2014} its condition, its two ratings, and the strain that can destroy it.

Gate status

Every character has a Gate, rolled at birth. Its status decides whether you can take mana in, push magic out, or neither.

StatusMeaning
OpenHealthy. The default for anyone born with a magic school.
Partly OpenFunctional but limited. The default for anyone born without magic.
DamagedStrained past its first threshold. Still works.
DestroyedStrained past its last threshold. You cannot cast or charge at all, and it does not heal.
DefectiveBorn broken, in one of two ways. Rolled with a small chance at birth regardless of magic.

Defective Gates

A Defective Gate is not simply a weak one \x{2014} it is broken in a specific direction, and which direction matters enormously.

VariantWhat breaks
No AbsorptionYou cannot draw mana in from the atmosphere. What you have is all you will ever have.
No ReleaseYou cannot push magic out. Spells from the six schools are refused outright \x{2014} including ones a spirit would have paid for, because they still leave through your Gate.
No Release is not the same as having no magic. Skills that merely cost mana still work. You can still commune with spirits and form contracts, both permanent and temporary \x{2014} only actual school magic is blocked.

Output and Efficiency

Two separate ratings, both rolled at birth, both raised by the Magic stat.

  • Output \x{2014} how much mana you can push through per tick. This sets your casting speed, and if a spell demands too much relative to your Output, the cast fails outright.
  • Efficiency \x{2014} how much of the mana you spend actually becomes spell. Low Efficiency does not stop you casting; it just makes everything cost more.

Strain

Strain accumulates when you overreach \x{2014} most commonly by holding mana above your capacity. It decays on its own over time, but while it is high it degrades the Gate itself: past one threshold the Gate becomes Damaged, and past the next it is Destroyed for good.

Destroyed is permanent. Nothing in normal play repairs a Destroyed Gate. Only a command can restore it.

Protections that change your Gate

Some Divine Protections set your Gate when you receive them. They set it once and then let go \x{2014} afterwards the Gate is ordinary stored state, so it can still be strained, damaged, destroyed, or changed by command like anyone else's.

ProtectionStamps
SorceryOpen, zero strain, maximum Output and Efficiency.
