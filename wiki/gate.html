The Gate

The organ every caster channels through. Its condition decides whether you can cast at all; its two ratings decide how fast and how cheaply.

Gate status

StatusMeaning
OpenHealthy. The starting state for anyone born with a magic school.
Partly OpenThe starting state for anyone born without magic.
DamagedStrained past 75. Still functional.
DestroyedStrained past 100. You cannot cast or charge mana at all.
DefectiveBorn broken, in one of two ways. A flat 5% chance at birth, regardless of magic.

Defective Gates

A Defective Gate is not a weak one — it is broken in a specific direction, and which direction matters enormously. The two are equally likely.

VariantWhat breaks
No AbsorptionYou cannot draw mana in from the atmosphere. What you have is all you will ever have.
No ReleaseYou cannot push magic out. Every school spell is refused — including ones a spirit would have paid for, because they still leave through your Gate.
No Release is not the same as having no magic. Skills that merely cost mana still work. You can still commune with spirits and form contracts, both permanent and temporary — only school magic is blocked.

Output and Efficiency

Two separate ratings, each rolled 1–10 at birth and each scaled up by the Magic stat (up to +75% at maximum).

  • Output — how much mana you can push per tick. This decides your casting speed, and whether the cast succeeds at all.
  • Efficiency — how much of the mana you spend actually becomes spell. Low Efficiency never blocks a cast; it just makes everything cost more.

Why a cast fizzles

Every spell has a mana per tick draw rate. If that rate divided by your effective Output reaches 3.0, the cast fails outright and the mana is wasted.

In practice: with Output 3 you can safely sustain a spell drawing up to 8 per tick, but a spell drawing 9 — like Al Shamak — will fail every time. Creative mode and a handful of spells that explicitly ignore Output are exempt.

Spell caps

Separately from Output, your Magic stat level caps the total mana of any spell you can cast on your own. Attempting something above your cap is refused before it begins.

TierMagic levelLargest spellUnlocks
Novice0–14250Shamak, Minya, Vita, Murak, Teleportation
Adept15–39500El Shamak, El Minya, Ul Shamak, El Vita
Expert40–79750E.M.M., Door Crossing, E.M.T.
Master80+1500Al Shamak, Al Karum, Ul Minya, Ol Shamak
A spirit can lift you over your own cap. Spirits carry their own spell ceiling, and casting is checked against the best available to you. This is how a low-level caster reaches a spell they could never fund alone.

Strain

Strain runs 0–100 and accumulates when you overreach. The main source is overcharging — holding mana above your maximum. You get 50% headroom above your cap for free; past that, every further 10% tier adds 1 strain per second.

Strain decays on its own over about 20 minutes. While it is high it degrades the Gate itself:

StrainResult
75Gate becomes Damaged (glass-break sound)
100Gate becomes Destroyed (warden heartbeat)
Destroyed is permanent. Nothing in normal play repairs it — strain decaying back to zero does not bring the Gate back. Only a command restores it.
Only Open and Damaged Gates degrade. Partly Open and Defective Gates are never pushed to Destroyed by strain. Creative mode and an active Lion's Heart both block strain gain entirely.

Protections that change your Gate

Some Divine Protections set your Gate when you receive them. They set it once and then let go — afterwards it is ordinary stored state, so it can still be strained, damaged, destroyed, or changed by command like anyone else's.
