Mod compatibility

Mods that HahUeuh has explicit handling for.

This page is still being written. The structure below is in place; the prose is not finished yet.

Explicitly handled

ModNotes
Distant HorizonsRolled-back chunks are pushed back into its LOD store.
CreateHandled during rollback.
Better CombatExplicit weapon attribute files so the mod's weapons are recognised.
Epic FightPlayer data attachments are preserved across a rollback.

A note on rollback and other mods

Return by Death restores files on disk. Mods that keep their own databases open while the server runs are skipped rather than corrupted.
