Getting started

How to install the mod, and what the world decides about your character in the first second after you join.

Installing

MinecraftLoaderMod version
1.21.1NeoForge2.6.1
1.20.1Forge2.6.1
  1. Install the loader that matches your Minecraft version.
  2. Put the HahUeuh jar in your mods folder.
  3. Launch the game once so the config files get written, then close it.
  4. Change anything you want in the config, then launch again.
Everyone needs the mod, including the server. This isn't a server-only mod. A player without it can't see the Book of Life, the ability bar or any of the visual effects, and will desync badly or get kicked.

Your first join

The moment you enter a world for the first time, the game rolls your character. This happens once and never again. Dying won't re-roll it, and neither will a Return by Death.

Here's what gets decided:

  • Your six stats. Each gets a hidden growth rate and a hidden ceiling. See Stats.
  • How long you live. Everyone starts Human, so somewhere between 80 and 120 years. This matters more than it sounds, because it sets how much mana you can hold.
  • Whether you know any magic. Most people don't.
  • Your Gate. Open if you were born with magic, Partly Open if you weren't. There's a small chance it comes out broken instead. See The Gate.
  • Your Divine Protections. See Divine Protections.

The odds, roughly

Both magic and blessings work the same way. You get one roll, and if it succeeds you get another, slightly harder one. It keeps going until a roll fails.

Being born with magic doesn't always mean you can cast. Only Yin has spells written for it so far. If you roll Fire, Water, Earth, Wind or Yang you get the school and an open Gate, but there's nothing in it to cast yet.

What to do first

Look at what you were given

Open the Book of Life and check your Gate and your stats. If your Gate says Partly Open and you have no schools, you're not a caster this life. Lean into whatever you did get instead.

Expect to lose progress

Return by Death is on by default. The world saves itself every minute or so, and when you die it goes back to that save. Not just you: the terrain, the mobs, the weather and everyone else on the server.

Read Return by Death before you build anything you would hate to lose.

Playing on your own is much gentler. The world only saves while somebody is online, so an empty server never drifts. In singleplayer you'll rarely lose more than a minute.

See if you can borrow magic

Even with no schools of your own you can still cast, by making a contract with a spirit. The spirit brings its own element and its own mana. Spirit Commune costs mana but doesn't need a school, so almost anyone can try it.

If something looks broken

What you're seeingWhat's probably going on
Config changes do nothing You're editing the wrong copy. On NeoForge the settings live inside the world folder, not the main config folder. See Configuration.
An ability vanished from your bar Slots clear themselves when you lose the protection or Authority that gave you the ability.
Bonus hearts empty every time you log in An old bug. Update the mod.
The game stutters every minute or so That was the world saving itself. Also fixed, so update.
