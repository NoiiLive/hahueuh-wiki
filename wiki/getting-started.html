Getting started

Installing the mod, and what the world decides about your character in the first second after you join.

Installation

MinecraftLoaderMod version
1.21.1NeoForge2.6.1
1.20.1Forge2.6.1
  1. Install the matching loader for your Minecraft version.
  2. Drop the HahUeuh jar into your mods folder.
  3. Launch once so the config files are written, then stop the game.
  4. Adjust anything you want in the config, and launch again.
Install it on the server and every client. HahUeuh is not server-only. A client without it cannot render the Book of Life, the ability HUD, or any of the Authority effects, and will be kicked or desync badly.

Your first join

The moment you first enter a world, the mod rolls your character. This happens once and is never repeated — dying, respawning, or even a Return by Death will not re-roll it.

  • Stats — six stats, each given a proficiency and a capacity from 1 to 10, plus a starting level. See Stats.
  • Lifespan — rolled within your race's range. Everyone starts Human; race only changes by command.
  • Magic — a chain of rolls decides how many schools you are born with. 75% of players get none.
  • The Gate — Open if you were born with magic, Partly Open if you were not, with a flat 5% chance of being Defective instead. Its Output and Efficiency are rolled 1–10. See The Gate.
  • Divine Protections — a chain of rolls at 25%, then 15%, then 5%. See Divine Protections.

The magic chain runs 25% for a first school, then 23%, 21%, 19%, 17%, 15% — but because the rolls compound it thins out fast. Roughly 1 in 5 players is born with one school, 1 in 22 with two, 1 in 102 with three, and 1 in 17,000 with all six.

Being born with magic may still leave you with nothing to cast. Only the Yin school has spells implemented. Rolling Fire, Water, Earth, Wind or Yang currently grants the school and opens your Gate, but there is no spell in it yet.

None of this is visible in chat beyond a couple of lines. To actually read your character, open the Book of Life.

What to do first

Check what you were born with

Open the Book of Life and look at your Gate and your stats. If your Gate is Partly Open and you have no schools, you are not a caster this life — put your points into what you did get.

Understand that you will rewind

Return by Death is on by default. The world checkpoints itself quietly as you play, and when you die it rolls back — not just you, but the terrain, the mobs, the weather and everyone else on the server. Read Return by Death before you build anything you would be upset to lose.

Playing solo? Return by Death is far more forgiving in singleplayer, because a checkpoint is only ever taken while someone is online. On an empty server the mod deliberately skips them.

Find out whether you can cast

Even with no schools of your own, you can still reach magic through spirits — a contracted spirit will fund spells from its own school and its own reserves. Spirit Commune costs mana but not a magic school, so it is open to nearly everyone.

If something looks wrong

SymptomLikely cause
Config changes do nothingServer-scoped configs live in the world folder, not the global config directory. You may be editing a stale file — see Configuration.
Abilities missing from the menuAbility slots prune themselves when you lose the protection or authority that granted them.
Bonus hearts vanish on rejoinFixed in recent versions. Update the mod.
The world stutters every few minutesThat was checkpoint capture running on the main thread. Fixed — update the mod.
