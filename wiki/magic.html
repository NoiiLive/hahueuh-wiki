Magic overview

Two reservoirs, one Gate, and a punishment for emptying the wrong one.

The six schools

Fire, Water, Earth, Wind, Yin and Yang. You may be born with them, be granted one by an affinity Divine Protection, or borrow one from a contracted spirit.

Only Yin has spells implemented. The other five schools exist and can be acquired every way listed above, but nothing has been written for them yet. See the spell list.

Mana and Od

These are not two names for the same bar. Mana is what you spend; Od is what you are.

ManaOd
Maximumlifespan × 2, then scaled by your Magic statlifespan × 5
RefillsYes — 10% per second while chargingOnly on death

Casting always drains mana first. If a spell needs more than you have left, the shortfall comes out of Od — and Od does not come back.

Spending your last Od cripples you permanently. Reaching zero Od inflicts the withered state and drives your Insanity straight to maximum. It is irreversible by design. Servers that consider this too harsh can set crippledEnabled = false, which instead refills Od and locks every magic skill for 20 minutes.

Why your race decides your ceiling

Because both reservoirs are derived from lifespan, and lifespan comes from race, the gap between races is enormous:

RaceLifespanBase manaOd
Human80–120160–240400–600
Half Elf300–500600–10001500–2500
Elf800–10001600–20004000–5000

A maxed Magic stat multiplies base mana by 2.5, and you may overcharge to twice your maximum on top of that. Even so, the biggest spells are out of reach for a short-lived character: Ol Shamak costs 1250, which a Human at maximum Magic can only meet by overcharging — and only at the top of the Human lifespan range.

This is what spirits are for. A contracted spirit brings its own pool and its own spell cap, which is how a Human ever casts anything expensive.

Charging

Holding the charge action draws ambient mana from the chunk you are standing in at 10% of your maximum per second. A chunk holds up to 5000 ambient mana and replenishes over about 3 days, so a heavily-farmed spot genuinely runs dry.

Charging past your maximum is overcharging. You get 50% headroom for free; beyond that you accumulate Gate strain. The hard ceiling is twice your maximum.

A No Absorption Gate cannot charge at all, and neither can a Destroyed one.

Casting

A cast has a total mana cost and a per-tick draw rate. Three separate things can stop it:

  1. Your Gate status — Destroyed blocks everything, No Release blocks all school magic.
  2. Your spell cap, set by Magic level, which refuses spells above a total mana threshold.
  3. Your Gate Output — if the per-tick rate is 3× your Output or worse, the cast fizzles and the mana is wasted.

All three are covered in detail on The Gate.

Where the mana comes from

Funding is resolved in sequence, not pooled: elemental spirit pools first, then a contracted spirit's reserves, then your own mana, then Od. Each source pays at its own Output and Efficiency, so one cast can genuinely be part spirit and part you.
