Named spirits

Two spirits that are characters rather than resources. They command several elements at once, and neither will ever turn up from an ordinary commune.

Both make the same trade. They cast extremely fast and extremely wastefully, which is the opposite of a well trained human caster. Expect spells to go off almost instantly and to cost far more mana than they should.

They also carry more magic per point of health than any ordinary spirit, so they're far deeper wells than even a Great spirit.

Puck

Four elements, the widest spread available to anyone. His Yin is deliberately the weak spot: he lends it at Quasi strength while everything else comes at Great.

Puck lives on a carried anchor and can pull mana out of the world around him rather than out of you. He also freezes the area near him.

Beatrice

Three elements, all at full strength, and no anchor needed. She's simply present rather than bound to an object.

Beatrice doesn't hold a pool of her own. She draws from her contractor, which means she's only as strong as you are.

Beatrice doesn't fight. She's a caster and nothing else. She will also fight back if you hit her, even if she's yours, so be careful with area attacks.

Getting one

Neither shows up from Spirit Commune. An admin has to hand them over: 

/rezero spirit <player> add named <puck|beatrice>

Servers can turn on a setting that limits each of them to one contractor at a time, but it's off unless someone enabled it.
