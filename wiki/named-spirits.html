Named spirits

Two spirits that are characters rather than resources. They command several schools at once, have fixed Gate ratings instead of rank-derived ones, and neither can be rolled from an ordinary commune.

Puck and Beatrice

Both trade the same way: enormous Output, miserable Efficiency. They cast fast and expensively, which is the opposite of a well-trained human caster.

Named spirits carry 30 mana per point of health, the highest ratio in the game — a Great spirit manages 20.

Puck

Four schools, the largest spread available. His Yin is deliberately weaker than the rest — he lends only Quasi rank there against Great in Fire, Water and Earth.

Puck is anchored to a carried item and can siphon mana from his surroundings, drawing 35% from the environment. He also freezes an area — 3 attempts within a 4 block radius.

Beatrice

Three schools, all at Great rank. Beatrice uses no anchor — she is present as herself rather than bound to an object.

Her Gate is fixed at 90% Output and 10% Efficiency, and she draws mana from her contractor rather than holding a pool of her own.

Beatrice is magic-only. She does not fight physically. Attacking her — even as her contractor — will make her retaliate.

Obtaining them

Neither appears from a normal Spirit Commune. They are granted by command: 

/rezero spirit <player> add named <puck|beatrice>

The main config has a singleNamedSpiritHolder switch that restricts each named spirit to one contractor server-wide. It is off by default.
