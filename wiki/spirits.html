Spirits overview

A contracted spirit lends you its school, its mana, and its own Gate ratings. For a character born without magic — three in four of them — this is the way in.

Ranks

Four ranks. Rank sets everything: the size of the spirit's mana pool, the biggest spell it can fund, and how much Output and Efficiency it lends you.

Gate share is a percentage of the maximum Output and Efficiency, rounded down — so at the default cap of 10 a Lesser spirit lends 2 of each and a Great spirit lends 8. Carried spirits of the same school add their shares together, so several small spirits stack rather than one overriding the rest.

Spirit spell caps mirror your own. A Great spirit's 1500 cap is the same as a Master-level caster's. This is how a low-level player reaches Al Karum or Ol Shamak — by contract rather than by training.

Spirit levels

Spirits level to a maximum of 100, and a spirit that levels far enough ranks up: Quasi at 15, Normal at 40, Great at 80. A Lesser spirit you keep alive is not permanently lesser.

Spirit Affinity

Affinity is a hidden stat rolled 1–10 at birth. It is judged as a percentage of the cap rather than as a raw number, and it is the single biggest factor in whether a commune succeeds.

The Divine Protection of Gathering Spirits raises your affinity cap and weights the roll toward rarer spirits.

Spirit Commune

Commune is a General skill — it costs mana but needs no magic school, so a No Release Gate can still use it. It costs 100 mana at 3 per tick, with a 10 minute cooldown, and ignores Gate Output so it can never fizzle.

The soft cap scales with affinity — 1 spirit at affinity 1, up to 6 at the affinity cap. Going over does not block a contract, it just makes each further one less likely. A high-affinity player can realistically reach about 11 spirits; someone on the 1% low-affinity rate is stopped by their first.

Casting with a spirit

When you cast, the mod funds the spell in sequence rather than pooling everything together: your own elemental pools first, then a contracted spirit's reserves, then your own mana. Each source pays at its own Output and Efficiency, so a cast can genuinely be part-funded by a spirit and part by you.

A spirit does not bypass your Gate. Magic funded by a spirit still leaves through you. A No Release Gate blocks it exactly as it blocks your own.
Distance matters. A carried spirit only assists within 16 blocks, and a spirit in an unloaded chunk can never assist at all no matter how close it appears to be.

Temporary contracts

Temporary Contract rolls several short-lived spirits at once. Each roll gets +5 percentage points over a permanent commune because the bond is fleeting, and temporary rolls ignore the crowding penalty entirely — so they stay viable when you already hold more spirits than your affinity really supports.
