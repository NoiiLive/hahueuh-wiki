Spirits overview

Contracting elemental spirits \x{2014} and casting through schools you were never born with.

Why spirits matter

A spirit lends you its school and its mana. This is the main route into magic for a character born without any \x{2014} you supply the intent, the spirit supplies the element.

Spirit Commune

Commune is a General skill, so it costs mana but needs no magic school. It rolls a chance of success influenced by your spirit affinity, how many spirits you already hold, and whether you are carrying miasma. On success you are offered a spirit, which you may accept or decline.

The Divine Protection of Gathering Spirits raises your affinity ceiling and weights the roll toward rarer spirits.

Casting with a spirit

When you cast, the mod funds the spell in sequence: your own elemental pools first, then a contracted spirit's reserves, then your own mana. Each source pays at its own Output and Efficiency rather than being averaged together.

A spirit does not bypass your Gate. Magic funded by a spirit still leaves through you. A No Release Gate blocks it exactly as it blocks your own.
