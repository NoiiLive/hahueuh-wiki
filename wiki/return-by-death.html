Return by Death

The world quietly saves itself while you play. When you die it goes back to that save, and you're the only one who knows it happened.

What actually happens

Every so often the game takes a snapshot of the world. When someone with this Authority dies, everything goes back to how it was at that snapshot. The blocks you broke are whole again. Mobs you killed are alive. The sun is back where it was, the weather is back to what it was, and every other player is standing where they were standing with the inventory they had.

You keep your memory. Nobody else does.

This happens to everyone, not just you. Every other player on the server loses whatever they did in that time too. On a busy server this is the single most important thing to understand about the mod.

When the world saves

Three things can trigger a save, and none of them is guaranteed. Each has a four in five chance of actually firing, so you can never be quite sure when your last one was.

TriggerHow often
A timerRoughly every minute
Earning an advancementWhen it happens
SleepingWhen it happens

Some things worth knowing:

  • Nothing saves while the server is empty. Your world won't quietly drift while nobody's playing.
  • A save waits if anyone has a chest or crafting table open, so it can't catch you halfway through moving items.
  • Saving runs in the background. If your game stutters every minute, you're on an old version and should update.
  • You aren't told when a save happens, though a server can turn that message on. It's genuinely helpful while you're learning the mod.

An admin can force a save at any time with /rezero checkpoint.

If a rewind seems not to have moved anyone, it probably just saved recently. The most common confusion here isn't a broken rewind, it's a save that happened five seconds before you died.

What doesn't get rewound

A few things deliberately survive, because rewinding them would either be pointless or would break something:

  • Your memory of the loop, and the Insanity that builds up alongside it. The cost of looping is meant to carry across lives.
  • Pocket dimensions. Door Crossing rooms and Al Shamak cells stay as they were.
  • The world's internal clock. Only the visible time of day moves back. Winding the real clock backwards breaks command blocks and every cooldown in the game.
  • Other mods' live data files, which are left alone rather than corrupted.

Witch's Scent

Looping leaves a mark on you. Witch's Scent builds up each time, to a maximum of five levels, and it isn't cosmetic: it makes spirits far less willing to deal with you, cutting your commune chances to about a third.

If you're trying to build a spirit contractor, dying a lot will actively work against you. Servers can switch this off.

What still kills you

Return by Death turns death into a reset, not into immunity. These resolve first:

  • Being sealed by Ol Shamak.
  • Being frozen by Lion's Heart.
  • Holding Phoenix, which revives you rather than letting the death happen at all.

Turning it down

Every part of this is adjustable. Each of the three triggers can be switched off on its own, the timer can be made slower or faster, the notification can be turned on, and the whole Authority can be disabled. See Configuration.
