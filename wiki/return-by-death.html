Return by Death

The world checkpoints itself as you play. When you die, everything rewinds \x{2014} and only you remember.

How it works

While players are online the mod quietly takes checkpoints of the world at a randomised interval. When a holder of Return by Death dies, the world is restored to the last checkpoint: terrain, block changes, entities, the time of day, the weather, and every other player's position and inventory.

You keep your memory of what happened. Nobody else does.

This affects everyone on the server. A rollback is not personal. Other players lose whatever they did since the checkpoint too.

Checkpoints

  • They are taken on a randomised timer, so the exact moment cannot be predicted or farmed.
  • No checkpoint is ever taken while the server is empty.
  • Capture runs in the background, so it should not stutter the game.

If a rollback appears not to have moved other players, the usual explanation is that the checkpoint was simply very recent \x{2014} not that the restore failed.

What survives a rewind

A handful of things are deliberately exempt, because rewinding them would be either meaningless or actively broken:

  • Your own memory of the loop, and the Insanity that comes with it.
  • Pocket dimensions \x{2014} Door Crossing rooms and Al Shamak cells.
  • The world's monotonic game time, so command blocks and cooldowns are not corrupted. Only the visible time of day is rewound.

Limits

Return by Death does not make you immortal in every sense \x{2014} it makes death a reset. Being sealed by Ol Shamak, frozen by Lion's Heart, or killed in a way the mod cannot cancel all still resolve first.

Turning it down

Everything above is configurable: the checkpoint interval, whether players are notified when one is taken, and whether the Authority is available at all. See Configuration.
