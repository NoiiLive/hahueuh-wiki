Authorities overview

An Authority is a family of active abilities granted as a set. They are not rolled at birth — they are earned, given, and fought over.

Variants

Greed and Sloth are not one power each. When the Authority is acquired a variant is rolled, and the variant decides what you actually get. They are not equally likely.

Mobs get a narrower roll. A mob given Greed can only ever be Lion's Heart. A mob given Sloth rolls all three variants with equal probability rather than by weight.

Compatibility

Both Greed and Sloth track a hidden compatibility score. You earn points by acting in the spirit of that Sin, and at 100 points the Authority becomes available to you.

AuthorityEarns points forPoints
GreedLooting a chest5
Mining ore2
Trading with a villager1
SlothLetting a tamed pet make a kill5
Sleeping through the night2
Travelling by vehicle1

Greed rewards accumulation; Sloth rewards letting something else do the work. Both scores can be read and set with commands.

You do not always start from zero. Starting compatibility is rolled between 0 and 100, so some characters are born most of the way to an Authority and others have the full climb ahead of them. The whole compatibility system can be switched off in the main config.

Witch Factors

Greed and Sloth each have an associated Witch Factor, tracked separately from the Authority itself. Holding a Witch Factor burns away every Divine Protection you have — the two can never coexist.

One holder at a time

The main config has a singleAuthorityHolder switch that revokes an Authority from its previous holder whenever it is granted to someone new. It is off by default — out of the box, any number of players may hold the same Authority at once. There is a matching singleNamedSpiritHolder for Puck and Beatrice, also off by default.

Actives that are not Authorities

Several powerful actives come from Divine Protections instead — Judge, Usurp, Tutoring, Impart Knowledge, Revitalize, Air Dash, Close Wound and Open Wound. Your magic schools and Spirit Arts also appear as their own categories, so everything you can actively do lives in one menu.
