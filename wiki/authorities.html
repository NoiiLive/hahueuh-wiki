Authorities overview

The Witch Cult\x{2019}s Authorities, and the ability sets each one unlocks.

What an Authority is

An Authority is not a single power but a family of active abilities, granted as a set. They are not rolled at birth \x{2014} they are given, taken, and fought over.

Alongside these, the ability menu also groups your magic schools and your Spirit Arts as categories, so everything you can actively do lives in one place.

Other active abilities

Several powerful actives come from Divine Protections rather than Authorities \x{2014} Judge, Usurp, Tutoring, Impart Knowledge, Revitalize, Air Dash, Close Wound and Open Wound among them. See Divine Protections.

Miscellaneous actives

Material Phase, Object Freeze, Ally Tracker, Base Shift, Second Shift, Book of Wisdom, Mental Overload, Vision of Danger and Vision of Life all exist as their own abilities.
