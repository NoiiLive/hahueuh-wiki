Authorities overview

The powers of the Witch Cult. Unlike blessings, these aren't handed to you at birth. You earn them by how you play, and other people can take them from you.

What's available

AuthorityWhat it is
Return by Death The world rewinds when you die.
Greed Comes in three flavours: Cor Leonis, Lion's Heart or Echidna.
Sloth Invisible hands. Also three flavours.
Domain Greed's Domain, tracked on its own.
Finger A piece of Sloth, handed to you by someone who already has it.

Variants, and why they matter so much

Greed and Sloth aren't single powers. When you get one, the game rolls which variant you receive, and that decides every ability you will ever have from it. Two players can both have Greed and share nothing at all.

AuthorityVariantHow likely
GreedCor Leonis65%
Lion's Heart30%
Echidna5%
SlothInvisible Providence55%
Unseen Hands40%
Sekhmet5%
Mobs roll differently. A mob with Greed is always Lion's Heart. A mob with Sloth gets an even chance of all three rather than following these odds.

Earning one

Greed and Sloth each track a hidden score based on how you play. Fill it up and the Authority becomes available to you. You're rewarded for acting in the spirit of that Sin.

Greed rewardsSloth rewards
Looting chestsLetting a pet make your kills
Mining oreSleeping through the night
Trading with villagersRiding rather than walking

Greed rewards piling things up. Sloth rewards getting something else to do the work. Chest looting and pet kills are worth the most, at five times what a villager trade or a boat ride gives you.

Some characters start most of the way there. Your starting score is random, anywhere from nothing to already complete. If an Authority becomes available suspiciously early, that's why. The whole system can be switched off by your server.

Witch Factors

Greed and Sloth each have a matching Witch Factor, tracked separately from the Authority itself. Picking one up destroys every Divine Protection you own, and you can never hold both at once.

Can two people have the same one?

By default, yes. Out of the box any number of players can hold the same Authority.

Servers that want these to feel rare can switch on a setting that takes the Authority off the previous holder whenever it's given to someone new. There's a matching setting for Puck and Beatrice. Both are off unless your server turned them on.

Abilities that aren't Authorities

Plenty of the best active abilities come from blessings instead, including Judge, Usurp, Tutoring, Impart Knowledge, Revitalize, Air Dash and the two wound abilities. Your magic schools and spirit abilities show up in the same menu, so everything you can actively do lives in one place.
