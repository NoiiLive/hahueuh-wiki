Witch Factors

The counterpart to a Divine Protection, and its opposite in every sense. You cannot hold both, and gaining one destroys the other.

Mutually exclusive with protections

Gaining a Witch Factor burns away every Divine Protection you hold. This is not a temporary suppression — the protections are gone. While you carry a Witch Factor you also cannot be granted or roll a new one.

Each Witch Factor is tied to an Authority. Greed and Sloth each have one, tracked separately from the Authority itself, and both are set by command.

By default a Witch Factor is not lost on death. Turn on loseWitchFactorOnDeath if you want it to be.

Witch Factor mobs

Mobs can carry Witch Factors, and by default they spawn naturally with a 1% chance. That spawn is sky-only — a factor mob will not appear underground or in a roofed dimension, which is determined by whether the dimension has a ceiling rather than by checking for the Nether by name.

A factor mob seeks out players across dimensions. Its movement speed and how often it re-targets are both configurable.

Miasma

Miasma is the residue magic leaves behind. It accumulates in the chunk you are in, capped at 500, and decays over about 3 days.

Once a chunk passes 30% of the cap, its miasma starts having effects. The most immediate consequence for a player is that carrying miasma cuts Spirit Commune success to 35% of what it would otherwise be — spirits do not like it.

The Divine Protection of Anti-Malignance makes you inert to miasma entirely.

Insanity

Insanity is a tiered madness that escalates to level 5 by default. Each episode lasts around 30 seconds at the base tier, and higher tiers strike far more often.

TierWhat starts happening
1–2Episodes only.
3+Episodes draw blood. Like poison, it can never drop you below half a heart.
4+A 35% chance an episode takes control of your body — the Bodily Disconnect flailing, without the blackout.

Each tier also draws miasma toward you, at 400 per tier.

Insanity survives a rollback on purpose. It is deliberately exempt from Return by Death, so the cost of looping accumulates across lives. Spending your last Od also drives it straight to maximum.
