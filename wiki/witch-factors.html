Witch Factors

The dark opposite of a blessing. You can never have both, and picking one up destroys everything you had.

They burn your blessings away

Gaining a Witch Factor destroys every Divine Protection you own. This isn't temporary. They're gone. While you carry a Witch Factor you also can't be given or roll a new one, so there's no rebuilding until the Factor is gone.

Each Witch Factor is tied to an Authority. Greed and Sloth both have one, tracked separately from the Authority itself, and both are handed out by command.

Dying doesn't get rid of one unless your server turned that on.

Mobs that carry them

Mobs can have Witch Factors too, and about one in a hundred spawns with one naturally. They only appear under open sky, so you won't meet one in a cave, and never in a roofed dimension like the Nether.

A Factor mob hunts players down, and it will follow you across dimensions to do it.

Miasma

Miasma is the residue magic leaves behind. It builds up in whatever chunk you're casting in and fades over roughly three in game days.

Every spell you cast adds a little. Spells you hold up, like E.M.M. and E.M.T., add a little every second they're running. Once a chunk gets about a third full it starts having effects.

Miasma is why your spirit recruiting dried up. Carrying it cuts your chance of a successful commune to roughly a third. Heavy casters struggle to gather spirits for exactly this reason. Move somewhere clean and try again.

The Anti-Malignance blessing makes miasma do nothing to you at all.

Insanity

Insanity is madness that gets worse in stages, up to five. Episodes last about thirty seconds at the bottom, and come much more often as it climbs.

StageWhat starts happening
1 to 2Episodes, and nothing worse.
3 and upEpisodes start hurting you. Like poison, it can't quite kill you.
4 and upEpisodes can take control of your body. Roughly a one in three chance each time.

Each stage also pulls miasma toward you, which makes the spirit problem above worse.

Insanity survives a rewind on purpose. Return by Death puts everything else back, but not this. The cost of looping is meant to follow you across lives. Emptying your Od also sends it straight to maximum.
