Anchors & contracts

Spirits live on a carried stone rather than following you around. Each anchor holds its own spirit and its own mana.

Anchors

The only valid anchor item is Glintstone. Binding a spirit writes it onto that specific stack, so the anchor is the contract — carry it and the spirit assists you, leave it in a chest and it does not.

A spirit only assists within 16 blocks, and a spirit in an unloaded chunk can never assist regardless of distance.

Carried anchors of the same school stack. Each contributes its rank's share of Output and Efficiency, so three Lesser fire spirits add up rather than one overriding the others.

Siphon modes

An anchor can be set to refill its spirit's pool from one of three sources. Use the anchor to cycle between them.

Managing spirits

The Manage Spirits ability opens the roster, where you can:

  • Rename a spirit.
  • Sever the contract, releasing it permanently.
  • Toggle individual abilities a spirit is allowed to contribute to.

Admins can also force matters with /rezero spirit <player> unanchor <all|name> and /rezero spirit <player> recall, the latter being the fix when a spirit is stranded in an unloaded chunk.

Temporary Contract

A General skill, so it needs no magic school and works with a No Release Gate. It rolls 1 to 5 spirits in a single cast, each lasting 3 minutes.

Temporary contracts ignore crowding entirely. The soft-cap penalty that makes each additional permanent spirit harder to get does not apply, so they stay viable no matter how many spirits you already hold. They also need no anchor — they simply expire.

Temporary spirits stack their mana pools and schools with everything else you have, which makes this the standard way to reach a spell that is normally out of range.
