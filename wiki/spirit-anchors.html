Anchors and contracts

Spirits live on a stone you carry rather than following you about. Lose the stone and you lose the spirit's help.

Anchors

The only thing that works as an anchor is Glintstone. Binding a spirit writes it onto that exact item, so the stone is the contract. Carry it and the spirit helps you. Leave it in a chest and it doesn't.

A spirit only helps within 16 blocks, and one sitting in an unloaded chunk can't help at all however close it looks.

Carrying several of the same element stacks them. Three Lesser fire spirits add together rather than the best one winning, so a pocketful of weak spirits is genuinely worth having.

Choosing how a spirit refills

Use the anchor to cycle through three settings. This is worth getting right, because the default will quietly eat your own mana.

SettingWhat it does
PlayerRefills the spirit out of your own mana, at half rate.
EnvironmentRefills it from the world instead. Puck only.
NoneLeaves the spirit to recover on its own.

Managing your spirits

The Manage Spirits ability opens your roster. From there you can rename a spirit, cut it loose for good, or control which of your abilities it's allowed to help pay for.

Admins have two useful commands. recall brings back a spirit stranded in an unloaded chunk, and unanchor frees one from its stone.

Temporary Contract

Temporary Contract doesn't need a school of magic, so it works even with a No Release Gate. It rolls anywhere from one to five spirits at once, and each of them sticks around for three minutes.

It costs 150 mana and has a five minute cooldown, and every roll is slightly more likely to succeed than a permanent commune would be.

Temporary spirits ignore crowding completely. The penalty that makes each extra permanent spirit harder to get doesn't apply here, so these stay reliable no matter how many you already have. They need no anchor either, they just expire.

Their mana and elements stack with everything else you're carrying, which makes this the usual way players reach a spell that's normally beyond them.
