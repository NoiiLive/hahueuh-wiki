Greed

Three entirely different Authorities wearing one name. Which one you get is rolled when you acquire Greed, and it decides every ability you will ever have.

The variant is everything. A Lion's Heart holder and an Echidna holder share nothing but the name. There is no overlap in their ability sets at all.

The three variants

VariantOddsTheme
Cor Leonis65%Allies — finding them, and shifting harm onto yourself.
Lion's Heart30%Freezing time — bodies, objects, and a heart placed in another.

A mob given Greed can only ever roll Lion's Heart.

Lion's Heart

AbilityCooldownEffect
Lion's Heart10s Freezes a body in time for 5–10 seconds — a randomised duration each use. This is a time freeze, not a heal or a buff: the target is simply removed from the flow of events.
Little King60s Places your heart in another, at up to 250 blocks. Hearts remember their position rather than tracking the entity, so they survive the target being unloaded.
Material Phase5s Requires an active Lion's Heart.
Object Freeze5s Requires an active Lion's Heart. Mobs use this with a 20% chance.
Only Ol Shamak may touch a frozen player. A body frozen by Lion's Heart is out of time. Nothing else in the mod can affect it, and a frozen player also cannot accumulate Gate strain.

Echidna

Echidna is built around a single object. Every other Echidna ability requires the Book of Wisdom to be summoned first — without it they refuse outright.

AbilityCooldownEffect
Book of Wisdom30sSummons or dismisses the book. The prerequisite for everything below.
Mental Overload20sNeeds the book.
Vision of Danger15sNeeds the book. A toggle.
Vision of Life15sNeeds the book. Reveals footprints, which linger 30 seconds.

Echidna also gets a passive outside the ability menu: enchanting is more efficient.

The Domain

Aldebaran's Domain is tracked as its own Authority rather than as a Greed variant ability, and has two modes:

  • Victim — the domain centres on you.
  • Aggressor — the domain is projected onto a target.

The Domain has its own checkpoint slot, separate from Return by Death, and rewinds on death by design. Its own config file controls it.

Earning Greed

Greed compatibility rises as you accumulate: 5 points for looting a chest, 2 for mining ore, 1 for a villager trade. At 100 the Authority becomes available.

Projectile abilities reach 32 blocks by default.
