Greed

Three completely different Authorities sharing one name. Which one you get is rolled when you receive Greed, and it decides everything you can do with it.

The variant is the whole thing. A Lion's Heart holder and an Echidna holder have nothing in common but the word Greed. There is no overlap in their abilities at all, so find out which one you have before reading further.

The three variants

VariantChanceWhat it's about
Cor Leonis65%Your allies. Finding them, and taking their pain for them.
Lion's Heart30%Stopping time. Bodies, objects, and putting your heart inside someone else.
Echidna5%Knowledge. A book, and the things it lets you see.

A mob with Greed is always Lion's Heart.

Lion's Heart

Lion's Heart

Freezes a body in time for somewhere between five and ten seconds, rolled fresh each time you use it. This isn't a heal or a buff, it takes the target out of events entirely. Ten second cooldown.

Nothing can touch a frozen player except Ol Shamak. A frozen body is outside time. Nothing else in the mod affects it, and a frozen player can't build Gate strain either.

Little King

Puts your heart inside someone else, up to 250 blocks away. The heart remembers where it is rather than following the target around, so it survives them wandering into unloaded chunks. One minute cooldown.

Material Phase and Object Freeze

Both need a Lion's Heart already active before they'll do anything. Five second cooldown each. Mobs with this variant use Object Freeze about one time in five.

Cor Leonis

The most common variant, built around protecting other people.

Ally Tracker

Shows you where your allies are, up to a hundred of them. Five second cooldown.

Base Shift

Moves harm onto yourself. Can't be used at the same time as Second Shift. 30 second cooldown.

Second Shift

Takes a share of an ally's status effects onto yourself, always at least a tenth of them.

Echidna

The rarest variant, and the one with a catch. Everything Echidna does runs through the Book of Wisdom, and the other three abilities flatly refuse to work unless the book is out.

Book of Wisdom

Summons or dismisses the book. Nothing else in this variant works without it. 30 second cooldown.

Mental Overload, Vision of Danger, Vision of Life

All three need the book out. Mental Overload has a 20 second cooldown, both Visions 15 seconds. Vision of Life reveals footprints, which hang around for half a minute.

Echidna also gets something outside the ability menu entirely: enchanting works better for you.

The Domain

Aldebaran's Domain is tracked as its own Authority rather than belonging to a variant. It has two modes: Victim, centred on you, and Aggressor, projected onto someone else.

The Domain keeps its own separate save of the world, apart from Return by Death, and rewinds when you die by design.

Earning Greed

Greed builds up as you accumulate things. Looting a chest is worth the most, mining ore counts for less, and trading with a villager counts for least. See Authorities.
