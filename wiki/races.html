Races and lifespan

Three races that live wildly different lengths of time, and an ageing clock that keeps running while you're offline.

You don't get to roll a race. Everyone starts Human. Nothing in normal play changes that, and only an admin command can. In practice race is a roleplay and server setup tool rather than something you're dealt at birth.

The three races

RaceLives for
Human80 to 120 years
Half Elf300 to 500 years
Elf800 to 1000 years

Your exact lifespan is picked once, somewhere in your race's range. If an admin changes your race, it gets picked again from the new range.

Why this matters more than it looks

Lifespan isn't just flavour. It's where your magical capacity comes from, so it quietly decides how good a caster you can ever be.

An Elf holds roughly eight times what a Human does. Since everybody starts Human, handing someone Elf or Half Elf is handing them an enormous magical advantage. Worth thinking about before you give it out on a server. See Magic.

Getting older

Everyone starts at 16 and gains a year every thirty in game days.

The clock runs on the world's calendar rather than your playtime, which means you age while you're logged off. Come back after a long break and all the years that passed get applied at once.

Ageing can be switched off completely in the config, which freezes everyone where they are.
