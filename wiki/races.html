Races & lifespan

Three races with wildly different lifespans — and an ageing clock that runs on the world's calendar rather than your playtime.

Race is not rolled. Every player starts as Human. Nothing in normal play changes that — race is set only by command, which makes it a roleplay and server-setup tool rather than something you are dealt at birth.

The three races

RaceLifespan range
Human80 – 120 years
Half Elf300 – 500 years
Elf800 – 1000 years

Your exact lifespan is rolled once within your race's range. Changing race by command re-rolls it against the new range.

Why lifespan is not just flavour

Lifespan is the single most important number a caster has, because both magical reservoirs are derived from it:

  • Maximum mana = lifespan × 2, then scaled by your Magic stat
  • Maximum Od = lifespan × 5
RaceBase manaOd
Human160–240400–600
Half Elf600–10001500–2500
Elf1600–20004000–5000
An Elf has roughly eight times a Human's magical capacity. Since everyone starts Human, granting Elf or Half Elf by command is effectively granting an enormous casting advantage — worth knowing before handing it out on a server. See Magic.

Ageing

Age starts at 16 and rises by one every 30 in-game days by default. The clock is measured against the overworld's day counter, not your personal playtime — so you age while logged off, and the missed years are applied the next time you join.

Ageing can be switched off entirely in the player config, which freezes every player at their current age.
